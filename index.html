Phase 3 · AI literacy rollout

Training week by week, on real work

No classroom theory. Every session uses tasks your people already do, so the time invested comes straight back. Everyone takes part, including leadership.

The time ask is small on purpose: about an hour a week per person, inside normal work. This is the typical shape; the real calendar is set with you. And since February 2025, the EU's AI rules (Article 4 of the AI Act) expect some AI training in every company whose staff use AI tools. Nothing dramatic, and a right-sized program like this is exactly the kind of step they ask for.
